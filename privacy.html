Spaceward Ho! Privacy and Terms

spacewardho.com · Version 5.1 (Alpha) · Last updated October 2026

The short version

  • You can play without an account. If you do, nothing about you leaves your browser.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies, and we never sell or share your information.
  • If you make an account, we keep only what's needed to sign you in, keep your saved games, and show your wins in the shared Hall of Fame.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it yourself, at any time.

Playing without an account

Your games, preferences and this computer's Hall of Fame are kept in your browser's own storage, on your device. We never receive them. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. It lets your saved games follow you to other devices and puts your games in the Hall of Fame that everyone sees. We use Google Firebase for this, with servers in the United States. Google processes the data for us and doesn't use it for advertising. When you're signed in, we keep:

  • Your sign-in: an account ID, plus your email address and name if you use Google or an email account. A guest account has only an ID and no personal information.
  • Your saved games, if "Keep my saved games in my account too" is on. Only you can read them.
  • Your Hall of Fame entries: the name you played under and the game's statistics (the year, difficulty, galaxy and so on). Everyone can see these. Names are checked by a decency filter, and any name that fails is shown as "Anonymous Cowboy".

We don't collect your location, contacts, device identifiers or browsing history.

Children

Spaceward Ho! is a strategy game for a general audience. Anyone can play without an account, and a guest account collects no personal information. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you're under 13, please play without an account or as a guest. If we learn that a child under 13 has given us an email address, we will delete that account. Parents can delete it themselves (below) or ask us to.

Deleting your data

  • Your account: open Account… from the title screen or the Ho menu and choose Delete my account and data. That deletes your account, its saved games and its Hall of Fame entries. Firebase may ask you to sign in again first.
  • Guest accounts are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Your browser's copy: clear this site's data in your browser's settings.

  • The game is free, and it's provided as it is, without warranties. It's an alpha, so it may have bugs, and saved games may occasionally be lost.
  • Please keep the names you choose decent. We may remove Hall of Fame entries, and accounts that abuse the shared features.
  • We may update this page; the date at the top says when it last changed.

Questions

Use the suggestion box in the game: open the Ho menu and choose Suggestion box. You can also delete everything yourself, as described above.
