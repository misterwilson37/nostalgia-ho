# Translation proofread — October 2026

Two rounds: a native-speaker-style proofread by Claude, then a second opinion from Gemini on all four languages, judged by Claude ("Gemini's second opinion", below). A native-speaker-style proofread of the remake's own French, German, Turkish and Spanish, asked for by another Claudling: `js/lang-remake.js` (the game's text) and `js/ui-text.js` (the interface), read against `docs/translations.md`. Official text in the versions that shipped it was not touched. Proofreader: Claude, working name **Marginalia**.

**90 changes to the default text**: 45 in the game's text, 45 in the interface (corrections of *official* wording are not among them: they are in "The patch table"). The hover help has 25 more ("Hover help", below). Interface strings are shared by every version unless the "Shown in" column says otherwise; game lines are listed with every version whose language table uses them (worked out from the built rulesets, `HO.RULESETS[*].lang[code]`, not by hand).

French and Turkish in iOS 1.2.9 and Android 1.2.8 are official, so no fix below touches them. Spanish is the remake's in every version; French and Turkish are the remake's from 3.0.1 on; German is the remake's in 1.2 and from 3.0.1 on.

## The patch table

Jake's rule, set after the rounds above: **a version plays its official translation as released, slips and all; an official translation's wording is also the default wherever the remake carries it; corrections of official wording are the patch's** (optional, New Game's unofficial patch). The remake's own text, which no player ever saw in any other form, is corrected in the default. So every correction of *official* wording, this work's and the older ones, now sits in a patch table, and the default shows the official wording again.

**Wired (October 2026, by the main Claudling).** The tables below were data the game didn't read when this was written; they are read now (the "Wired" paragraph after the list). They were:

- the game's text: `js/lang-remake.js` `fr.patch`, `tr.patch` (English → corrected line, each with its reason as a comment) and `tr.patchWords` (a filling). The engine already has the mechanism: `inLanguage` (`js/engine.js`) lays `rs.lang[code].fix[id]` over a language when the patch is on and `rs.fixes` lists `id`, as iOS's `trLostWord` and `trSpiesReport` do. `js/languages.js` has to build that `fix` from these tables for the remake's languages (every version but iOS and Android, whose own fixes stay), and each version's `rs.fixes` needs the entry (its title and text shown in New Game's list and the Patch notes; `docs/fixes.md`).
- the interface: a corrected row of `js/ui-text.js` has a seventh element, `{ lang: corrected }`, ignored by `HOUI.add` today. `js/ui-lang.js` has no patch yet: `HOUI.tr` would take that element when the game in progress has the patch on (outside a game, the title screen, there is no game and no patch).

**What was done when it was wired.** Game text: `js/languages.js` builds `rs.lang[code].fix.translations` from `fr.patch`, `tr.patch` and `tr.patchWords` for every version that shows a line with an entry (the translator's `fix`, `patchFix`; hints too, as `hintTexts`), wherever the version shows the official line or the remake's line that follows it; `engine.js` `inLanguage` lays it over when the patch is on and `rs.fixes` lists `translations` (`E.TRANSLATIONS_FIX`, in 3.0.1's, 4.0.5's, Mac 4.0.5's, 5.0.5's list, and so the Palm's, iOS's and Android's); a fix's `say.words` now merge over the language's. The interface: `HOUI.set(lang, patch, words)` and the rows' seventh element (`js/ui-lang.js`; `syncLang` in the classic page passes `HO.fixed(G, 'translations')`). iOS and Android get the same fix for their own lines and, by Jake's decision, play their placeholder order (`BY_PLACE`) as released: the patch's `iosFillings` puts French 36 and Turkish 11, 38 and 84 right. Entries in the game's text: French 3 (the 2 below and 3.0.1F's Ship joke "Tout le monde"), Turkish 22 and 1 word; the interface 6 rows. Per version (reports + hints): 3.0.1 Turkish 7, Windows 4.0.5 Turkish 10 + 1, Mac 4.0.5 Turkish 11 + 1, 5.0.5 French 2 + 1 and Turkish 14 + 1, the Palm French 1 and Turkish 16 + 3, iOS and Android French 1 and Turkish 9 (and the fix's word), each plus the Ship joke in French where it shows it.

The old `over` tables (French 1 line, Turkish 11) were the remake's corrections of broken official lines, applied by default; they are now the `patch` tables' first entries, and `over` is empty, so `languages.js` already shows the official lines. `node tools/lang-test.js` (every language, every ruleset) and `--adapted` pass, `ui-lang-audit.js` passes (classic, mac4, palm, ios: 20 runs).

("you explored"); the spies report as the line-of-credit report; French 5.0.5's lost battle as "You  et vos alliésavez perdu une bataille àArgot. …". ## Option C, French (chunk 2)

By Concordance, after chunk 1. The French follows the same policy as the Turkish (preservation by default, the best French in the patch), with rule 4 deciding which official French each version uses. The word list is **`docs/glossary-fr.md`**. The core words agree across 1.2F, 3.0.1F and iOS (Eclaireur, Croiseur, Transport, Satellite; Distance, Vitesse, Armes, Boucliers, Mini) and the remake's French already used them; what differs is the names of menus, windows and features, which are each translation's own.

**Jake's answers in this chunk** (October 2026):

- **5.0.5 and the Palm take iOS's French first** (iOS, Android and the Palm are ports of 5.0.5: the same game), 3.0.1F's where iOS has none: the main Claudling's routing, as it was. (Rule 4's earlier wording, "lean on 3.0.1F", is replaced. A first pass of this chunk had put 3.0.1F first: 18 lines each differ, mostly 3.0.1F's own phrasings and its spacing before "!"; it was undone.)
- **3.0.1F's window words in 4.0.5 too** ("makes perfect sense"), as in 3.0.1.
- **New Game's French follows the skin chosen at startup**, leaning to iOS's: "Nouveau Jeu" by default, 3.0.1F's "Nouveau" in a 3.0.1 or 4.0.5 game or on such a skin's title screen; the patch says "Nouvelle partie".

## The patch for iOS's Turkish (chunk 3)

By Claude, working name **Cedilla**. Jake's rule, restated for this chunk: **the unpatched game plays as close to the original as possible, so iOS's Turkish is broken in it exactly as it shipped, in every version that carries it; the patched game is the best Turkish possible**, iOS's own lines included, and every clumsy line, not only the broken ones (a redundant "Sen"/"Senin", iOS's "siz" in a "sen" game, a suffix on a number). Nothing in the default changed: the same seeds play the same messages, word for word, before and after (iOS, 5.0.5, 4.0.5, 3.0.1, patch off), and the interface's default cells are untouched.

**Game text** (`js/lang-remake.js` `tr.patch`, 290 entries, each with a comment naming iOS's list and index and what it has wrong): 155 of iOS's 159 reports, 36 of its 39 alerts, 41 of its 42 hints (the first three are 5.0.5's opening messages as iOS words them), 2 Evacuate questions, 10 whole-line Misc Strings (the battle verdicts, Name a Star, Armageddon, the Hope joke), the 19 Radical Research programs, and 27 of the versions' own lines that keep iOS's wording (adapted or carried). The worst of iOS's: report 72 says you *lost* the metal that fell on the star; 14 that the colony does *not* belong to the rebels' owner; 24 that the fleet stopped and then went on; 77 and 46 drop the name; 129 "next game" for next turn; 21 a "hot hole" for a wormhole; alert 23 asks you to *prove* you are online; hints 20-24 call planets satellites. The patch's Turkish is "sen" throughout, drops the subject pronoun Turkish doesn't need, and says the glossary's word for every game thing (`docs/glossary-tr.md`, with chunk 3's new rows).

**Case endings** (new, the patch only): `js/engine.js` `trSuffixes`, called by `say` and the battle windows. A patch line writes `%s’{e}`, `{de}`, `{den}`, `{deki}`, `{i}`, `{in}`, `{le}` after a filling and the ending is fitted by vowel and consonant harmony ("Argot’a", "Sirius’tan", "3’ü"); `{^}` capitalises the next letter. The official lines have no tokens, so they keep iOS's glued endings ("Argotye").

**Allies.** The filling "and your allies " (Misc Strings 39, "Ve Müttefikleri ", "and *his* allies") is " müttefiklerinle birlikte" in the patch, and the battle reports put it where Turkish takes it ("Argot’ta müttefiklerinle birlikte bir savaş kazandın. Gemilerinin 3 tanesini müttefiklerinle birlikte kaybettin."), so no line reads "Sen … Sen" when you have none. It replaces chunk 1's `patchWords` entry; `patchWords` now holds the remake's two Send Message groups.

**iOS's name lists** (new: `tr.patchLists`, iOS list → index → the patch's Turkish; `js/languages.js` `LP`, `patchesLists`). Laid over iOS's lists when the patch is on, in iOS and Android (`iosLang`'s fix), 5.0.5 and the Palm (`build505`), 4.0.5 and 3.0.1 (`buildText`): the class names (and the singular after a number, "12 Savaşçı", where iOS has "12 Savaşcılar"), the decoy, the fillings "Your enemies" and "and your allies ", about 70 tech names, the tech kinds in report 85, the battle window's templates (iOS's `%d ölü %ss` appends an English "s": "Savaşs", "İzcis", as released) and Name a Star (Misc Strings 74 "İlerdeki oyunlada"). 3.0.1's and 4.0.5's battle window (`words20`) takes the same names. `patchFix` takes an `extra` (say.args, say.words, battlePanel, nameAStar) for this.
