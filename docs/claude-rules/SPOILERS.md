# Spoilers: the jokes and easter eggs

All written from the manual's voice. None was copied from the original games.

**Galaxy names** (the manual's rejected titles, "feel free to pick the one you like best"):
- **Corral the Stars:** every star is named after a frontier town.
- **Stars and Spurs:** everybody starts at Speed 2.
- **Space Cowboys of the 21st Century:** the game starts in 2001, as any pedant will tell you.
- **Stellar Conquest:** every computer is a warmonger.
- **Frontier MacSpace:** Peter called it "Space".
- **Star Command:** taken by Farallon, so the galaxy is renamed Spaceward Ho!
- **The Ho:** you're now really cool.

**Names**
- A galaxy or player named **Bonehead** gets Bonehead Directions tips and a dunce-cap welcome.
- Choosing **Diabolical**, or naming yourself Joe or Peter, brings **Joe and Peter** into the game ("Try playing against Joe and Peter"). If you're Joe or Peter yourself: "Hey, that's our name!"
- Computers sometimes copy your ship names: "Six-Shooter (Not a Copy)".

**Radical duds**
A self-stirring coffee mug, a square tumbleweed, a 73rd degree, ground combat (uninvented after reading the Why chapter), radar that only sees fleets that already arrived, and more.

**Radical discoveries with flavor**
- The Tao of Pooh: research is calmer and slightly sticky.
- Pirated technology, with a reminder from Delta Tao's stand on copy protection.
- Mind control: the victim's message calls their fleet traitors.
- Biologicals: please don't feed them after midnight.

**Events and milestones**
- A senator actually got a letter (research boost).
- A cosmic ray renames a star "Somebody Else's Fault". Our software never crashes.
- Settling Mars beats 1968.
- A 1.00 G, 72° world calls for a hammock.
- Betelgeuse finally goes, and astronomers owe each other money.
- The year 3000: humanity thanks you. (We told you so. —Delta Tao)
- Debt over $6 million brings the national-debt joke.
- Bankruptcy: please put down the blender.
- Scrapping: no styrofoam peanuts were harmed.
- Winning renames your capital "<Name>'s Star", for the stars you name for winning.

**Computer chat**
- They greet you by personality.
- They answer your messages: "Let's be allies", "I hate you", "I need money", and so on.
- Swearing gets "Language! There are children in this galaxy."
- They taunt you when they attack, curse when they lose, and quote the manual when you betray them ("Hatred and distrust, permanently. It says so in the manual.").
