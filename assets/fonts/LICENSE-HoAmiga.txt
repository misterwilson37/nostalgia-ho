HoAmiga-Regular, HoAmiga-Bold, HoAmigaSmall-Regular and their Tall twins
(HoAmigaTall-*, HoAmigaTallSmall-*; woff2) are the X Window System's Adobe Helvetica bitmaps at 75 dpi (helvR12, helvR10 and
helvR08, font-adobe-75dpi, from Debian's xfonts-75dpi 1.0.5), each pixel
drawn as a square (HoAmiga) or as a rectangle half as wide as it is tall
(HoAmigaTall), the Bold ones emboldened by one pixel, by tools/amiga-font.py,
for the Spaceward Ho! web remake's Amiga skin. They are renamed: Helvetica is
a trademark of Linotype; these are only its 1987 screen bitmaps. The notice
