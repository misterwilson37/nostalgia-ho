palmos-turkish.ttf, palmos-bold-turkish.ttf: the six Turkish letters (G-breve, dotted I, dotless i, S-cedilla) in Palm OS's standard and bold 11 px fonts, taken from the Turkish language
database of Interpilot 2.5 (Sergey Menshikov, sergem.net; discontinued, its files offered free to download "at your own risk") by tools/extract/winfon.py. Palm OS itself had no Turkish letters.
