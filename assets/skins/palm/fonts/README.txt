palmos.ttf, palmos-bold.ttf: the Palm OS standard and bold fonts, 11 px, one square per ink pixel (tools/extract/winfon.py, from a Windows .FON of them).
They are Damien Guard's FontStructions "Palm OS" (https://fontstruct.com/fontstructions/show/653622) and "Palm OS Bold" (https://fontstruct.com/fontstructions/show/653643; cloned from "Palm OS"):
the .FON's glyphs match them (ink pixels equal in 93 of 94 letters; checked 19 Nov 2026), so these two files are licensed as they are, under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0
licence (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Their notices, as FontStruct supplied them, are LICENSE-palm-os.txt and LICENSE-palm-os-bold.txt beside this file.
