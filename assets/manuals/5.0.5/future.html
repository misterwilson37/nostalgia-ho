Future Directions

Spaceward Ho! is likely to go through some revision over the next couple of years, but it will strive to continue to be simple and fun. We welcome suggestions -- many things suggested by early users are now in the game. Suggestions that make things simpler and more fun are most likely to be implemented.

The best way to give us suggestions for the Ho! is in writing. When you talk to us on the phone, it has to get translated too many times before it gets to the programmer for us to really tell what the suggester had in mind.
