Recommended Reading

These are a couple of books that don't have anything to do with Macintosh software, but we enjoy reading them time and again. On top of that, we think they say something about the Delta Tao philosophy.

The Tao of Pooh , by Benjamin Hoff, Penguin Books, New York, 1982. The best book on Taoism ever.

Calvin and Hobbes , by Bill Watterson, Andrews and McMeel, Kansas City, 1987.

The Macintosh Way , by Guy Kawasaki, Scott, Foresman and Company, Glenview, Illinois, 1989.

Surely You Must Be Joking, Mr. Feynman, by Richard Feynman.
