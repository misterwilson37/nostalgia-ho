About Peter Commons

“There’s nothing worse than a fanatic plant eater,” says Joe Williams of Delta Tao, “but this one sure can write a good game.” Peter says he’ll be happy to write games for the Mac as long as they pay the rent: “Spreadsheet writers can’t have as much fun debugging their code as I do.” In real life, Peter is pursuing a master’s degree in computer science at Stanford (not Caltech). Oh well, nobody’s perfect. Spaceward Ho! is Peter’s first major Mac application.
