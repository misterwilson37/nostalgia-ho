Exploring Planets: Temperature & Gravity

At some stage you’ll want to leave your home planet and spend some effort exploring the nearby stars looking for other habitable planets. Each planet has two important characteristics: Temperature and Gravity.

Temperature represents everything you can change about a star system, from the atmospheric content to the planetary ecology to whatever. Your home planet is 72°F. You can spend money on terraforming new planets you colonize which will make their temperature get closer to 72°. When the temperature reaches 72°, the planet is as good for your habitation as it’s going to get.

Gravity represents everything you can’t change about a star system, from the output of the sun to the tidal forces of the moons. Some places just aren’t as habitable as others. Your home planet is 1.00 G, and the closer a planet is to that, the better off the population will be. In an normal game, planets bigger than 2.0 G or smaller than 0.5 G will never be able to make a profit and thus are not worth colonizing for the long term. Of course, metal being the scarce commodity it is, you shouldn’t balk at colonizing an inhospitable planet long enough to take all its metal.
