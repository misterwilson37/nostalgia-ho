Glossary

Current Resources
The money and metal currently available for use.
Enemy
Anybody that isn’t you. You have no formal allies in this game.
Gravity
Everything that can’t be changed about a star. A planet between 0.5 G and 2.0 G can be made profitable, if enough people are on it and it is fully terraformed. An ideal planet is 1.0 G. Each player has a different idea of what 1.0 G is.
Income Last Turn
The amount of money (ΔMoney) or metal (ΔMetal) you amassed last turn.
Metal
The sum of all nonrenewable resources.
Mini
The amount of shrinking a ship has. High Mini ships use less metal, but cost more money.
Money
The sum of all replenishable resources. You get money every turn from profitable planets.
Planet
The same thing as a star.
Range
How far a ship can reach without refueling. A Range 6 ship can go out 3 and back 3, or can go out 5 and be stuck.
Satellite
A ship that can’t move. Either its Range or its Speed (hopefully both) are zero.
Shield
Something that protects a ship. A Shield Tech 8 ship will prove invincible to a Weapon Tech 4 ship.
Speed
How fast a ship goes. A Speed 3 ship will take 2 turns to go to a star that is 5 units away.
Star
The same as a planet.
Temperature
Everything that can be changed about a star. The ideal temperature is 72°. Each player has a different idea of what 72° is.
Terraforming
Improving the temperature of a star. When a star is fully terraformed, its temperature is 72°.
Weapon
Something a ship shoots. A Weapon Tech 8 ship will cut through Shield Tech 4 ships like a hot knife through butter.
