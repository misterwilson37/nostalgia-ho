Recommended Reading

These are a couple of books that don’t have anything to do with Macintosh software, but we enjoy reading them time and time again. On top of that, we think they say something about the Delta Tao philosophy.

  • The Tao of Pooh, by Benjamin Hoff, Penguin Books, New York, 1982.
  • Calvin and Hobbes, by Bill Watterson, Andrews and McMeel, Kansas City, 1987.
  • The Macintosh Way, by Guy Kawasaki, Scott, Foresman and Company, Glenview, Illinois, 1989.
  • The Art of War, by Sun Tzu, various versions available.
Spaceward Ho! Manual (Macintosh, 1990) — from Jake Wilson’s own copy, bought from PC Games Vault (pcgamesvault.com), scanned at 300 dpi; the figures cut from the scans.
