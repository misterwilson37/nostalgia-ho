Foreword

When the Mac first came out, it was to be an appliance. It was small, so it could be parked innocuously in a kitchen, like a toaster. Apple envisioned a Macintosh in every home. Alas, it was not to be. Yet.

We who did buy Macs for our homes wanted, among other things, to play games on them. Unfortunately, there has been a real shortage of quality Macintosh games from the beginning. There are a lot of good reasons for this, but the main one is this: Writing Mac games is a lot of work for very little money.

It’s sad that a bad PC game makes more money than a good Mac game. We think a lot of this is a chicken and egg problem — There weren’t many good games for the Mac, so nobody bought a Mac to play games. Nobody buys Mac games, so nobody writes them.

We love the Mac, and love computer games, so we decided to bite the bullet and write some games for the Mac. Even if our games don’t make a profit, they need to be done, just to spur the Mac into the homes, where it belongs. Besides, we wanted to play them. In this frame of mind, we wrote Spaceward Ho!.
