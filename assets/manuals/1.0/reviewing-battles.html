Reviewing Battles

Sometimes your ships bump into other players’ ships. When this happens, they fight until one side is dead. Admittedly, this doesn’t make for easy peaceful coexistence, but this is a war game. Anyway, when you have a fight, you’ll want to know what the heck happened. There’s a couple of ways to find out. First, if you have “Always Review Battles” checked in the “Options” menu, you’ll get a summary of whatever battles just happened at the beginning of each turn.

You can also select any star and review the last battle at that star by selecting “Review Battle at this Star” from the “Galaxy” menu. If there have been two or more battles at a star, you’ll only be able to see the most recent one.

You can review the battles graphically or just as a report by checking or unchecking “Graphic Battles” in the “Options” menu. Non-graphic battles will appear as text in the Report Window. Graphic battles are not meant to show realistically how the battle went — just what happened. Showing animated ship-to-ship combat would be neat, but it’s not really what this game is all about.

If you lose a battle, you don’t get a report about the last round of the battle, since your ships were destroyed before they could send in a final report. This means that if your fleet was destroyed in the first round, you’ll have no idea how many enemy ships were there — just that there were enough to annihilate you.
