About Spaceward Ho!

Spaceward Ho! is a game of space exploration and conquest for one to ten players. Each player controls the economy and military of a spacefaring race.

The most important part of Spaceward Ho! is its simplicity. Controlling every aspect of an alien race could be very complicated and could get in the way of the fun of the game. Fun is our number one goal, and we have sacrificed realism at every turn when such a sacrifice could be made. If you want realism in space exploration, play “Let’s Cut NASA’s Budget” like they do in Congress every year.

Spaceward Ho!’s name has gone through a lot of changes. When we first thought of the game, we called it Star Command. It turned out that that name was taken by Farallon — Star Command is software that controls Star networks. Peter called it Space, an abbreviation for what the rest of us called Frontier MacSpace. John Lade hit upon the cowboy planet idea somewhere along that time, and we liked it. We didn’t want to use Frontier MacSpace in real life, because we were considering porting it to the home video games. (Call us capitalists. Go ahead.) The week we had to know the name for printing reasons, we all brainstormed, and came up with several creative names. Feel free to pick the one you like best: Corral the Stars, Space Cowboys of the 21st Century, Stellar Conquest, Stars and Spurs, and, of course, Spaceward Ho!
