Moving Ships

Ships can only be moved as part of a fleet. To move a fleet, first select the planet where the fleet is located. Then select the fleet you want to move in the Planet Window. Now click on the star where the fleet is located on the Star Map and drag to where you want the fleet to go. If the destination is reachable (either directly or by refueling along the way at colonies), the path will be shown by a solid line (or lines). Letting go of the mouse when pointing at a reachable destination orders that fleet to go to the specified star. If the destination cannot be reached, the path will be shown by a gray line. This line is for informational purposes only; a fleet will never accept those orders as they are “impossible.” To prevent a fleet from moving, click and hold on the planet where the fleet is located for at least a second. A fleets’ course cannot be changed while it’s between planets.
