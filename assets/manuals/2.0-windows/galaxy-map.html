Galaxy Map

The Galaxy Map

The right part of the main window of Spaceward Ho! is the Star Map. It lets you see where you are, where you've been, and where you can go.

The type of star, or who owns it, is depicted in the way the star appears on the map. When you start the game, all the stars are unexplored except for your home planet, which is shown as a planet with a cowboy hat. As you colonize other planets, they too are shown with hats. When you encounter enemies, the stars that are owned by them will be identified by the icon of the enemy. Planets which have been explored, but not colonized, appear as either nice, blue places that are probably good for colonization, or brown, dirty orbs that are probably not (but may be good for strip mining).

The currently selected star is highlighted by a green corona. All the planet information to the left of the star map will now refer to that planet.
