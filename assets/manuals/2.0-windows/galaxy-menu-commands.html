Galaxy Menu Commands

Compare Players... This will show your ratings in various areas against all of the remaining players in the game.

List Explored Stars... Presents a list of all the stars you have visited, and gives a summary of the data relating to each star.

Send Message... Allows you to send a message to another player. The messages take the form of "I like" or "I don't like" another player or a planet. Or, you can send the message "I own..." and name one of the planets that you currently colonize. This will inform another player of your whereabouts.

Zoom In Zooms in for a closer look at the star map.

Zoom Out Zooms out so you can see more of the map at one time. When you've backed off completely, the stars appear as simple disks and there are no names.

Fix Spending If you have any spending problems, such as not allocating enough funds or allocating too many, this option will fix things up for the current turn.
