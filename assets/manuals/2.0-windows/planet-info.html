Planet Information

The information about the currently selected star includes the current income, population, temperature, gravity and metal resources to be found there. If this planet is not yet fully terraformed and mined, you will see a bar chart to the right of this information. This bar chart shows how much of the money spent on this planet is to be used in the areas of Terraforming, Mining, and Shipbuilding. You can change the amounts spent in each category by clicking on the desired bar and setting it where you like.

Below the planet information is a list of the fleets that are stationed there, and their status.
