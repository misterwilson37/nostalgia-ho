Adjusting the Budget Bar Charts

The most important controls you have in Spaceward Ho! are your bar charts. They are what you use to divide up your spending and implement your strategy.

You can adjust these bar charts by clicking on them and dragging them to the desired position.

The Budget Information bar chart is what you use to divvy up your money between each of your colonies. You can also use some of it for technology research, or put some into a savings account (with interest!).

The Tech Window bar charts appear in the Tech Window and are used to divide up the emphasis given to various areas of technology research.
