Sound, sound cards, and the internal speaker

The Ho! sound, sound cards, and the internal speaker

Spaceward Ho! utilizes several sound effects during game play. You can turn the sound on or off by using the Sound option under the Options menu.

Sound hardware supported includes all Multimedia compatible waveform sound devices under Windows 3.1 (or Windows 3.0 with Multimedia extensions). Support for the SoundBlasterTM card is also provided for Windows 3.0.

If you do not have a compatible sound card, sound is output through the internal speaker. Sound quality will vary, depending upon the speed of your system and the design of your internal speaker. Sound from the internal speaker is also affected by mouse or keyboard activity during playback.
