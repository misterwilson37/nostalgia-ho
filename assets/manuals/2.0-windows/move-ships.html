Move Ships

How to Move Ships:

You move a fleet in Spaceward Ho! by first selecting the fleet in the Fleet List that you wish to move. Then, click on the selected star and drag the line to the desired destination. If the fleet can reach the destination and return, the line appears as an arrow with points at each end. If the fleet can only make the journey one-way, the arrow appears with a single point at the end. If the fleet cannot reach the chosen destination at all, the line appears as a grey line with no arrow tips. The distance (in light years) between the stars appears as a number alongside the line. Release the button to give the fleet its orders. You will hear a "Hyaah!" sound letting you know the course is set (assuming that the Sound Option is on). A fleet which has been dispatched will also appear in bold print in the Fleet List.

To make a fleet hold still, or cancel its move, click on the planet where the fleet is located and hold it for a second. When you've done it right, you'll hear a "Whoa!" sound when you let up the mouse (if the Sound Option is on), and the fleet will no longer be in bold print in the Fleet List.
