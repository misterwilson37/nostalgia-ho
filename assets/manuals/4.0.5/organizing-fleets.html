Ships

Organizing Fleets

Sometimes you may want to divide a fleet into pieces or put several fleets together. To do this, select Organize Ships from the "Ships" menu. Then click and drag the ships around until they're distributed the way you want them. Sorry, a fleet can only contain ships of the same type (to reduce file size).
