SPACEWARD HO! - CHANGES FROM 2.0 TO 3.0

Spaceward Ho! - Changes from 2.0 to 3.0

Spending is now based on income, rather than total money supply. Ships get built the turn you ask for them, instead of the old shipbuilding queue. Money is allocated to your planets automatically, so you can't accidentally underspend on them. The Graph History chart has been added, so you can see your long-term trends. Several gratuitous graphics and sounds have been added. Some new galaxy types have also been added.

Alliances have been added, so you don't have to kill everybody to win. New messages have been added, and now the computer players deal with friends and enemies. The computers now send semi-intelligent messages to everyone. You can give money and metal to your friends (or enemies), or surrender to them.
