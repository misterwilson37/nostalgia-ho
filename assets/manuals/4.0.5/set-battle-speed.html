The Options Menu

Set Battle Speed

You can control the speed of shots and explosions in the battle reviews by setting the battle speed here. If you get bored watching battles, speed them up. Having Group Shoot Together will speed up battles considerably, especially with giant battles. Deselecting Fully Animated Battles will skip the explosion graphics, making battles go even faster.
