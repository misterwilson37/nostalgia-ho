Changes and Additions

Spaceward Ho! - Additions from 3.0 to 4.0

There's an awful lot of new stuff in version 4.0 of the Ho! If you're familiar with 3.0, you'll be able to play with the new version and figure everything out. However, the following is a handy list of new features so you'll know what you're getting.

Several new radical techs have been added, and the old ones have been adjusted for better game balance. Three new ship types have been added - Dreadnoughts, Tankers, and Biologicals. Several dialogs have been revamped, especially ship building.

You can now turn the battle luck off as you start a new game so no players will ever receive the black cat or horseshoe in battle. Tons of new graphics and sounds have been added. You can now tell a planet's gravity by looking at it's picture - bigger planets are higher in gravity. Also, you can tell if a blue planet is hot or cold in the Star Map. Satellites have been made twice as hard to kill, and shoot twice every round. They also cost twice as much money and metal to build. Fix spending happens automatically when you end your turn, so it isn't in the menus anymore.

You now set your mining/terraforming ratio with a slider bar instead of the old pie chart. You can see the planet's picture before you've terraformed a planet. Click on the picture to see its details.

Ho! now keeps statistics on how you've done throughout the game, as well as stats on how a player is progressing over several games, with a "Master Point" scheme. Every time you win a game you get "Master Points" based on the difficulty of the game. Rack up as many as you can; harder games are worth many more points.

The new system rates the difficulty of the game on a rough 0-100 scale. Final difficulty depends on the number of allies, speed of game, and so on. You can now send private messages to other players, but it takes a turn for the message to arrive, just like other messages.

A chess-style turn clock has also been added to speed up play. Computer players are now listed in the order of income in the icon list. Also, diabolical computer players are even smarter, so watch out. They love Dreadnoughts.

There are now two levels of alliances - normal and best buddies. Also, late game joiners can take over for a computer player. You'll notice that in a network game turns update many times faster than they used to. One player games update faster, too. Real time chatting as well as free-form messages for private communication have been added.

Every turn you'll get hints on playing Spaceward Ho!. You can turn them off in the Preferences dialog once you think you've seen them all.

New Easter eggs - but we can't tell you about them here. You'll find them, or hear about them on the net. You'll also be able to find software updates on Spaceward Ho! (as they are released) on America Online (keyword: delta tao) or on the ftp at sumex.aim.stanford.edu (or a variety of others).
