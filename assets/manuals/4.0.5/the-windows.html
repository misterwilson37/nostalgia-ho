Playing Spaceward Ho!

The Windows

Spaceward Ho! has three windows. You can drag the windows around and resize them how you like, so you can configure the game for your monitor and style of play. Spaceward Ho! automatically saves your window layout when you quit and restores it the next time you play, so you don't have to keep fooling with it.
