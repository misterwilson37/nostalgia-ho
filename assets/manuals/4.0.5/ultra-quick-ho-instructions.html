Getting Started

Ultra-Quick Ho! Instructions

Here's the one paragraph summary of what you need to know. Put Spaceward Ho! 4.0 on your hard drive. Play with the spending bars to adjust spending. Click on the messages in the map to make them go away. Double-click on a colony to design and build ships. Drag from one star to another to move ships. Experiment. Conquer the galaxy.
