Other Players

Alliances

You can play the Ho! as a xenophobe, killing everyone you see. Or, if you feel friendly, you can be neutral toward other players (so you don't attack them without cause) or even have a strong alliance in which you share information with your friends. Of course, to form an alliance, both players have to want it. You show your opinion of the other players with the Alliances dialog in the "Galaxy" menu. This is also where you see their opinion of you.

There are now two levels of alliances. If both players choose "Best Buddies" alliances with each other, they will automatically share information on all their planets and exploration. Computer players never choose to be Best Buddies, so this is only for network play.

Neutral allies just don't kill each other. Friendly allies automatically receive information about the galaxy from their friends. You might also give them money and metal when they need it most. Do this with the Give Money/Metal from the "Galaxy" menu.

You can break an alliance by moving the alien in question from the "Like" column to the "Don't Like" column in the Alliances dialog. You'll now fight to the death everywhere you coexisted with him. Coincidentally, you'll also earn his permanent hatred and distrust.
