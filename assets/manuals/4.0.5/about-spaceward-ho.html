About Spaceward Ho!

The most important part of Spaceward Ho! is its simplicity. Controlling every aspect of an alien race could be complicated and could get in the way of the fun of the game. Fun is the number one goal, and realism has been sacrificed for fun everywhere possible. If you want realism in space exploration, play "Let's Cut NASA's Budget" like they do in Congress every year.
