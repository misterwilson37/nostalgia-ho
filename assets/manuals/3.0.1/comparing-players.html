Comparing Players

If you ever get the urge to see how you’re doing compared to the other players in the game, select “Compare Players...” from the “Galaxy” menu. You will get to see how some of your attributes (like Weapon Tech or Income) compare to others.

“Ship Power” is how big and tough your fleets are. It’s the sum of all the tech levels of all your ships. It can give you an indication of how powerful your fleets are.

The number of players in the game will not include anyone who is dead.

Click on the “Show Skills...” button to show what each player’s skill level is: novice, beginner, normal, expert, or advanced. Computer players will list a skill randomly chosen from among the various players’ skills, to help try to hide who the computer players are. The actual computer intelligence is at the bottom of this window.

The History Graph

This shows a 1,000 year history of your progress. You can turn on and off the various bars with the icons at the bottom of the graph. The technology graphs show your position relative to the other players, just like the “Compare Players...” dialog. The other three are “absolute” indicators—the more money you make, the higher you go on the graph, regardless of how well the other players are doing.
